# Rulebook
This is your rulebook: your own record of how things in this game work. It starts empty. Whenever you find out how an object or a mechanic behaves, write it down here, in whatever form you like: one line per finding, what you saw and how sure you are. Mark guesses as guesses, test them, then confirm, fix or delete them.

`rulebook_add(text)` adds lines to the end; `rulebook_write(text)` replaces the whole rulebook (to tidy it, merge lines or drop guesses that turned out wrong). The rulebook is shown here at the top of every turn and survives context cuts. Your notes are for everything else (progress, places, people).
